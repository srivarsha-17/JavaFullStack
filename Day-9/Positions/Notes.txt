POSITION: 

i. static : 
    default position(The top, right ,, bottom , left and z-index has no effect)



ii. absolute : 
    positioned relative to its closest positioned ancestor(removed form the flow)


iii. relative:
    element is relative to itself(the top, right, bottom , left and zz index will work )


iv. fixed : 
    positioned relative to browser(remove from flow)

v. sticky : 
    positined based on the users scroll position



z-index : 
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
It decides the stack level of elements
Overlapping elements with a larger z-index cover those with a smaller one.


z-index : auto (0)
z-index : 1/2/3
z-index : -1 / -2/ -3
